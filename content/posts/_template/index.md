Write in Markdown. Inline maths uses single dollars, like $\hat{\xi}$ or $\chi = \lim_{q \to 1} P(V > q \mid U > q)$.

Display maths uses double dollars:

$$
C(u, v) = \psi\!\left\{ \left[ \psi^{-1}(u) + \psi^{-1}(v) \right] A\!\left( \frac{\psi^{-1}(u)}{\psi^{-1}(u) + \psi^{-1}(v)} \right) \right\}
$$
