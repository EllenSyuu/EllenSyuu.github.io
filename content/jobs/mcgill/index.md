- Developed covariate-dependent Archimax copulas and a new maximum-likelihood estimator for their Pickands dependence function, supervised by Prof. Johanna G. Nešlehová.
- Analysed 88 years (1930–2017) of precipitation extremes at six stations in southern Québec with non-stationary GEV margins and year-block bootstrap intervals.
- Packaged the method as a tested R package with CI and a one-command (`make all`) pipeline that regenerates every table and figure from raw data.
