- Developed covariate-dependent Archimax copulas and a new maximum-likelihood estimator for their Pickands dependence function, supervised by Prof. Johanna G. Nešlehová.
- Analysed 88 years (1930–2017) of precipitation extremes at six stations in southern Québec: a pooled non-stationary GEV model cut parameters from 60 to 20 with comparable fit, capturing seasonality and a regime shift around 1975.
- In 150 Monte Carlo replications, the new estimator's mean integrated squared error was 44% and 91% lower than the CFG and Pickands estimators. Funded by an NSERC research grant.
- Packaged the method as a tested R package with CI and a one-command (`make all`) pipeline that regenerates every table and figure from raw data.
