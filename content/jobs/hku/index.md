- Analysed linked vaccination, infection and hospital records for 7.17 million Hong Kong residents with recurrent-event models, working with the School of Public Health.
- Found that a bivalent booster after three CoronaVac doses lowered BA.4 infection risk by 34.9% (HR 0.651) versus an original-strain fourth dose; co-authored the resulting paper in _Vaccines_ (2024).
- Final-year project (STAT4799, 2023–24, supervised by Dr. Eddy K. F. Lam): compared protection against Omicron reinfection across vaccination–infection histories (Comirnaty vs CoronaVac, two to four doses) with a discrete-time Andersen–Gill-type hazard model on territory-wide linked records — later rebuilt as the synthetic-data package [VVI](/#case-vvi).
