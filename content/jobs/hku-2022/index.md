- **Summer Research Fellowship** (Faculty of Science), supervised by Prof. Stephen M. S. Lee, Statistics & Actuarial Science: reviewed the semi-supervised learning literature and implemented a semi-supervised algorithm for the linear projection of a mean regression function.
- **Department of Social Work and Social Administration:** on an Early Career Scheme–funded, eight-year follow-up study of self-employment in ageing China, identified 24 variables and collected and analysed census data. Helped build the team’s shared database of China’s official statistics — about 11,000 files from seven statistical yearbook series (2001–2021) and all seven national censuses (1953–2020), from national down to county level — collecting and archiving the volumes and converting CD-ROM and scanned editions into Excel.
- **Sau Po Centre on Ageing:** designed Likert-scale questionnaires and analysed the responses for transdisciplinary studies on care robotics and family caregiving.
